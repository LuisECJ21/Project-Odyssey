# Project Odyssey

## What is Project Odyssey
Project Odyssey is the project of my professional life.

Its purpose is to transform me into an elite cybersecurity engineer with a strong specialization in offensive security. It is not only a learning journey, but a commitment to continuous improvement through discipline, engineering, and hands-on experience.

Throughout this journey, I will design and build a security platform that integrates both my previous experience and everything I learn along the way. The platform will serve as a practical environment to simplify, automate, validate, and continuously improve security processes.

Odyssey does not end when the platform is finished. It continues as both the engineer and the platform evolve together, expanding their capabilities, adapting to new technologies, and addressing increasingly complex challenges.


## Why does it exist?

Project Odyssey exists because it represents a dream that has been with me since I graduated from university. For years, I wanted to become a cybersecurity professional, and after many changes in my career, life—and God—gave me the opportunity to make that dream a reality.

At the same time, I have always wanted to create something meaningful. Not just another project, but something that could grow, evolve, and make a real difference. Cybersecurity became the field where those two ambitions came together.

Odyssey is the commitment to pursue excellence as an offensive security professional while building a platform that transforms knowledge into practical solutions. Every concept I learn, every challenge I overcome, and every mistake I make will become part of that platform.

This project is not only about becoming a better engineer. It is about building something that grows with me, continuously adapts to new threats and technologies, and ultimately helps organizations and people improve their security through knowledge, automation, and innovation.

"Ultimately, Project Odyssey is not the platform itself. It is the lifelong process of becoming the engineer capable of building it."


## Who am I today?

I am a curious, committed, and self-taught engineer who has chosen to reinvent himself through cybersecurity.

Although I have years of experience in technology, entering the cybersecurity world feels like the beginning of a new journey. It challenges me every day, pushes me to keep learning, and reminds me that there is always something new to discover.

Cybersecurity makes me feel younger. It gives me the excitement to spend hours researching a new topic, understanding how things work, experimenting in my lab, or simply reading about the latest threats and innovations—not because I have to, but because I genuinely enjoy it.

Today, I am not defined by everything I know, but by my willingness to keep learning, building, and becoming better every single day.
